How I work

This is how I work through a design task. It comes from my Advanced Diploma coursework in Australia.

Brief and scope

I start by writing down the scope, the limits and what a finished design has to show, then plan the work with review points and a schedule. I plan team work in writing: who owns each task, what "done" means, and how every assumption can be traced.

Where I did this
Advanced Diploma coursework, Feb 2024 – Feb 2026

Site data

I start with real data: published traffic volumes, geological maps, council drainage records and service searches. I make my own site evidence: I mapped vegetation from satellite data and drew the existing services from a Before You Dig Australia response.

Where I did this
Advanced Diploma coursework, Feb 2024 – Feb 2026

Standards

Before I start designing, I list the standards and guides that apply to the task. Across my coursework these included Austroads road and pavement guides, AS 1742.14 for traffic signals, AS 1726 and AS 1289 for site investigation and soil testing, AS 4100 and AS/NZS 1170 for steel and structural loads, and ISO 31000 for risk.

Where I did this
Advanced Diploma coursework, Feb 2024 – Feb 2026

Options

I compare more than one option and write down why I chose one over the others. In my coursework I did this for pavement treatments, intersection layouts, investigation plans and footing types.

Where I did this
Advanced Diploma coursework, Feb 2024 – Feb 2026

Calculations and costs

I back the chosen option with hand calculations, quantities and a cost estimate or bill of quantities, so someone else can check it. I work structures by hand, using equilibrium and the method of joints, and show the results as clear force diagrams.

Where I did this
Advanced Diploma coursework, Feb 2024 – Feb 2026

Risk and review

I keep a risk register and rate each risk by likelihood and consequence. I check the design against the brief, date my revisions, and write down my assumptions and sources so they can be checked. I revise my work through several versions and act on feedback.

Where I did this
Advanced Diploma coursework, Feb 2024 – Feb 2026
