CH 0+400Long section 01

How I work

I like to know where every number comes from. On a road I start from the traffic counts; on a building, from the load path and the soil. I compare options side by side, write down why I rejected the others, and keep my sources visible so another engineer can check my working.

Step 01 of 06

Brief and scope

I start by writing down the scope, the limits and what a finished design has to show, then plan the work with review points and a schedule.

On site, I planned daily work, communicated priorities and monitored progress for a crew of 10–15 workers.

Evidence
Advanced Diploma coursework, 2024–2026
Project-in-Charge Engineer, JustBuilt

Step 02 of 06

Real site data first

Before designing, I collect real inputs: published traffic counts, geological maps, drainage information, and Before You Dig Australia enquiries, which I lodged myself during coursework.

Evidence
Advanced Diploma coursework, 2024–2026

Step 03 of 06

Standards before design

I list the standards and guides that control a design before I start: Austroads road and pavement guides, AS 1742.14 for traffic signals, AS 1726 and AS 1289 for site investigation and soil testing, AS 4100 and AS/NZS 1170 for steel and structural loads, and ISO 31000 for risk.

On site, I reviewed structural and architectural drawings and checked work against project requirements.

Evidence
Advanced Diploma coursework, 2024–2026
Project-in-Charge Engineer, JustBuilt

Step 04 of 06

Options, side by side

I develop at least three options and record why each one is kept or rejected, whether it is a pavement treatment, an intersection layout, an investigation plan or a footing type.

Evidence
Advanced Diploma coursework, 2024–2026

Step 05 of 06

Calculate, measure, cost

I back the chosen option with hand calculations, quantities and a cost estimate or bill of quantities, so the recommendation can be checked.

On site, I tracked procurement and materials and kept material records.

Evidence
Advanced Diploma coursework, 2024–2026
Field Engineer, Grand Travaux

Step 06 of 06

Risk, review, revise

I rate risks by likelihood and consequence in a register, check the design against the brief, and revise through dated versions, keeping my assumptions and sources visible.

On site, I prepared site reports, material records and progress documentation for project monitoring.

Evidence
Advanced Diploma coursework, 2024–2026
Field Engineer, Grand Travaux
